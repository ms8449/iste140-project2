Which assistant for which task?

For finding current information with sources, Perplexity is the most useful, because every answer shows links you can check. For explaining a concept or getting feedback on your own writing, ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all work well. Gemini fits students who use Google Docs, while Copilot suits students who work in Microsoft Word. Whichever one you use, check important facts yourself, because all of them can make mistakes.

What you should never paste into an AI assistant

Never paste personal data, such as your address, ID number or passwords. Never paste exam questions, because that usually breaks your university's or schools academic integrity rules. Do not paste other people's work, such as a classmate's essay or code, because it is not yours to share. Turn off training in the privacy settings if you do not want your chats used. A simple rule: if you would not post it publicly, do not paste it into a chatbot.
